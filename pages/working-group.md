## Working Group

The **4D Seismic Hub Working Group** oversees the curation and development of this project. Its purpose is to ensure that contributions are accurate, properly attributed, and consistent with the goals of the community. 

Membership is open to anyone with a sustained interest in time-lapse seismic who is willing to volunteer time to review pull requests, attend occasional meetings, and help steer the project.

### Mission

*   Foster an open and collaborative environment for sharing 4D seismic knowledge.
*   Maintain a high-quality repository of open presentations, case studies, and tutorials.
*   Encourage best practices in acquisition, processing, and interpretation.
*   Provide a neutral forum where operators, service companies, and academics can exchange ideas.

### Structure and Membership

The working group operates by consensus. All members are equal. We typically meet quarterly via teleconference to discuss the direction of the hub and review new content submissions.
