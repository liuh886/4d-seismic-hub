## Decision context

Jubarte is a deepwater heavy-oil field in Brazil's Campos Basin. Complex connectivity and a moving water front created a direct development risk: a well placed from the static model alone could encounter water earlier than expected.

## Monitoring approach

Petrobras installed a permanent fiber-optic reservoir-monitoring system on the seabed. Keeping the receiver geometry fixed reduced a major source of non-repeatability and enabled faster repeat surveys than conventional redeployment.

## Evidence

- **Repeatability:** The permanent installation produced a highly repeatable 4D signal, with the benchmark value maintained in the shared project dataset.
- **Water-front imaging:** Time-lapse interpretation mapped water movement in a setting where heavy oil and complex connectivity made the response difficult to predict from the reservoir model alone.
- **Turnaround:** The workflow supported a shorter interval between acquisition and an interpretable 4D volume.

## Operational outcome

The interpretation showed water encroachment that was not sufficiently represented in the earlier model. The field team used this evidence to reposition a production well.

## Transferable lesson

A permanent system should not be justified by repeatability alone. Its strongest case is where better repeatability shortens the decision loop and protects a high-value well, injection, or intervention decision.
