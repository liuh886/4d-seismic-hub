### Project Overview
The Weyburn-Midale CO2 Monitoring and Storage Project in Canada uses CO2 for Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR). 

### Spot Seismic Innovation
In 2021, Whitecap Resources and SpotLight Earth introduced "Spot Seismic" to this site.
- **Concept:** Instead of full-field 4D surveys, they use sparse, targeted source-receiver pairs to monitor specific areas ("spots").
- **Impact:** High-temporal resolution monitoring at a significantly lower cost and environmental footprint.
- **Result:** Proved the effectiveness of sparse monitoring for long-term surveillance.
