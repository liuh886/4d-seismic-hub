### Project Overview
The Sleipner project in the Norwegian North Sea is the world's first industrial-scale CO2 storage project. Since 1996, over 20 million tonnes of CO2 have been injected into the Utsira saline aquifer.

### 4D Seismic Contribution
4D seismic has been the primary tool for verifying the safety and integrity of the storage. 
- **Impact:** Proved that CO2 remains trapped within the aquifer.
- **Key Takeaway:** Successfully quantified the distribution and thickness of individual CO2 layers using time-lapse amplitude analysis.
